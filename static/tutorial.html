01 What It Does

This platform takes a photo of a person and a dialogue script and produces AI-generated video clips where the subject appears to speak your words with natural lip-sync and head movement.

🎬

Talking-Head Videos

A single photo + script produces a realistic video of that person speaking.

🎭

Multi-Scene Stories

Multiple images + a full script creates transitions between different speakers or locations.

🌍

Multi-Language

English, Italian, Spanish, French, German — each with adapted speaking rates.

🎙️

Voice Cloning

After export, swap the AI voice with a real cloned voice via OpenVoice or ElevenLabs.

💡
The platform breaks your script into clips (~8s each), generates them in parallel, lets you review and approve, then combines everything into a final video.

02 First-Time Setup

Videos generate through your own Flow Worker — browser automation that runs on your computer using your Google account. No API keys needed. Set it up once, then keep it running while you generate.

Set Up the Flow Worker

  1. Click 🖥️ My Worker in the navigation bar
  2. Set the number of Chrome Windows (accounts) — each runs in parallel for faster generation. Start with 1.
  3. Click the big ▶ Start button (the orb)
  4. A .bat installer downloads automatically — double-click KavenoBuilder-Worker-Setup.bat
  5. Chrome opens — sign into your Google account (or select an existing logged-in profile)
  6. The worker auto-connects and the My Worker page shows ● Online
  7. Start generating jobs — the worker picks them up automatically
ℹ️
Multi-account: Set 2+ Chrome Windows for parallel generation. Each window uses its own Google account. Sessions auto-refresh every 6 clips (golden restore) to prevent detection.
⚠️
Keep the worker running while jobs process. Minimize Chrome but don't close it. The worker handles ghost detection, auto-redo, and HTTP downloads automatically.

03 Upload Images

Drag and drop photos into the upload area, or click to browse. Accepted: PNG, JPG, WebP.

ImagesModeWhat Happens
1 imageSingle Image ModeSame photo used for every clip. Best for single-speaker talking heads.
2+ imagesMulti-Image ModeUnlocks Auto-Cycle and Storyboard for multi-scene content.
💡
Well-lit, front-facing photos produce the most natural results. Clean or neutral backgrounds help the AI focus on the face.

05 Dialogue Script

Type or paste the words your subject will speak. Each numbered line in the editor becomes one video clip.

Auto-Split (Recommended)

The ✂️ toggle is on by default. Paste a full paragraph and the platform automatically breaks it into clips at sentence boundaries, sized correctly for your language and speaking rate.

Manual Mode

Turn auto-split off and press Enter to define clip breaks yourself. Each line = one clip.

⚠️
Line length matters. Too many words for the selected duration = audio gets cut off. The editor highlights problematic lines. Shorten those lines or increase the duration.
💡
Write the way people actually speak — contractions ("I'm", "don't"), natural sentence lengths, numbers spelled out ("twenty-five" not "25"). The AI needs speech patterns, not formal written text.

06 Generate

Click 🚀 Start Generation. The platform validates your config, uploads reference frames to cloud storage, generates AI prompts (if enabled), then begins parallel clip processing.

StatusMeaning
PendingWaiting in queue
GeneratingAI is actively creating this clip
CompletedDone — ready for review
FailedSomething went wrong — can be retried
Redo QueuedScheduled for regeneration
ℹ️
Each clip takes roughly 1–3 minutes. Clips process in parallel — a 10-clip job finishes in roughly the time of 2–3 clips sequentially.

07 Review Clips

Completed clips appear as cards with video previews. Watch each one and take action:

ButtonWhat It Does
✓ ApproveMarks as final. Green border appears. This clip is included in export.
↻ RedoRegenerates (up to 5 attempts). Browse versions with ◀ ▶ and pick the best before approving.
🗑 DeletePermanently removes this clip from the job.
💡
Don't wait for all clips to finish. Approve good ones immediately and Redo bad ones right away — redos process alongside remaining clips in parallel.

08 Export

📥 Download All

Downloads each approved clip as a separate MP4 for your own editing.

✂️ Export Final

Merges all approved clips into one continuous video. Opens Export Settings dialog.

Export Settings

SettingDefaultWhat It Does
Trim Start Frames0Frames cut from start of each clip. Removes static opening frames.
Trim End Frames7Frames cut from end of each clip (~0.3s at 24fps). Removes trailing artifacts and creates smoother clip joins.
Smart TrimONSkips start-frame trimming for the first clip and for clips at scene cuts. Preserves natural video openings.

Remove Silence

The 🔇 Remove Silence toggle runs Voice Activity Detection on the video and cuts quiet gaps between words.

ControlDefaultEffect
Trigger (min gap)0.3sOnly gaps longer than this are removed. Lower = more aggressive cutting.
Keep (padding)0.2sSilence preserved at each cut point for natural breath pauses.
ModeEnergy / WhisperEnergy detects loudness gaps; Whisper times gaps from the transcription.
Sensitivity0.75Cut aggressiveness from lenient (0) to strict (1).

Speed Up

The ⚡ Speed Up toggle increases playback speed from 1.01× to 1.50×. Makes content feel more energetic. 1.10× is recommended — barely noticeable but shaves seconds off total runtime.

Lineup Editor

Click ✏️ Lineup to open the drag-and-drop reorder interface. Rearrange clips before export without changing the original clip_index assignments. The custom order is saved and used by Export Final.

Audio Enhancement

ToggleDefaultWhat It Does
😂 Remove LaughterOFF (first run)Suppresses laughter artifacts and background noise using RNNoise. Adjustable strength (50–100%). Your choice is remembered after the first export.
🎛️ DeepFilterNetOFF (first run)Removes hiss and static while preserving voice clarity. Uses adaptive FFT denoising.
🎚️ Voice FilterOFFCompressor, noise gate, limiter. Can sound robotic — use only if audio needs heavy processing.
📊 Loudness NormOFF (first run)Normalizes volume to broadcast standard (-16 LUFS) for consistent playback.
ℹ️
Settings are remembered. Your export settings are saved automatically so you don't need to re-configure on every export. The success banner after export shows exactly which settings were applied.

09 Single vs Multi-Image Mode

Single Image (1 photo)

The simplest path. Upload one image, write your script, generate. Every clip uses the same image. Perfect for monologue-style content — spokesperson, explainer, interview.

Multi-Image (2+ photos)

🔄 Auto-Cycle

  • Images rotate round-robin through clips
  • Clip 1 → Image 1, Clip 2 → Image 2, etc.
  • Loops back after the last image
  • Easiest multi-image approach

🎬 Storyboard

  • Full manual control over scenes
  • Drag images into script to create scene breaks
  • Each scene spans multiple clips
  • Control clip mode and transitions per scene

10 Storyboard Mode

Drag an image into the script to create a scene divider. All clips within that scene use that image as their visual reference.

Clip Modes

ModeWhat HappensUse When
BLENDEvery clip starts from the original photoMost scenes — visually consistent
CONTINUEEach clip starts from the last frame of the previous clipLong monologues — subject pose carries over naturally
FRESHEach clip uses the original photo, no interpolationClips you plan to edit independently

Scene Transitions

🎬 BLEND TO

Last clip of the previous scene smoothly morphs toward the next scene's image. Cinematic and smooth.

✂️ CUT TO

Hard switch — no visual blending. Use when transitioning between very different people or settings.

💡
Start with BLEND mode + BLEND TO transitions for the smoothest results. Switch to CUT TO only when scenes are very different visually.

11 Voice Swap

After export, the success banner shows a 🎤 Voice button. Click it to replace the AI voice with a cloned real voice.

🎙️ OpenVoice v2

  • Low cost (~$0.01/run)
  • Upload a voice sample or use approved clips as reference
  • Adjustable similarity and pitch normalization

🔊 ElevenLabs

  • Premium quality
  • Requires ElevenLabs API key + Voice ID
  • Fine-grained controls: stability, similarity, style
  • Saved Voices: Save presets (name + key + settings) for one-click reuse
💡
Use a 5–15 second clip of clean, noise-free speech as your voice reference. Cleaner reference audio = better clone quality.

12 Attach Clips (Prompt Jobs)

Use 📝 Prompts Only to create a job with dialogue lines and prompts — without generating videos. Then upload clips you generated externally.

  1. Create a job with Prompts Only backend — prompts are generated but no video.
  2. In the prompts view, click 📎 Attach Clips.
  3. Drop your MP4 files into the upload zone.
  4. Choose matching mode: 🎤 By Voiceover (default, recommended) transcribes each clip and fuzzy-matches to your dialogue lines. 📁 By Filename sorts alphabetically.
  5. Click Attach — clips are uploaded to R2, matched to dialogue lines, and ready for review/export.
💡
Voiceover matching uses Whisper transcription and achieves 90–100% accuracy on Veo-generated clips. Always prefer this over filename mode.

14 Images Tab

The 🖼️ Images tab (top bar, next to 🎬 Videos) generates still images with Google's Nano Banana and Imagen models. Use it to build the visual frames for a video, or to make standalone images. When every scene is picked, you can promote the whole set straight into a talking-head video job.

🎬

Videos Tab

Talking-head video clips with lip-sync, rendered by Veo.

🖼️

Images Tab

Still frames generated by Nano Banana / Imagen. Pick the best, then promote to a video.

🧩

One Pipeline

The same markdown scene table drives both. Generate frames first, approve them, then make the video.

Layout

Switch to the tab and the action bar swaps to image tools — the main one is 📋 Import. The screen splits in two: the left panel holds the worker status and your job list; the right panel shows the selected scene's generated variants.

ℹ️
Images run on your own browser worker (see Image Worker), the same idea as the video Flow worker but pointed at Nano Banana.

15 Scene-Table Import

This is the main way to start an image job. Click 📋 Import to open the scene-table modal.

  1. Paste a markdown scene table into the box, or click 📎 Upload .md file. Each ### Scene N block becomes one image to generate.
  2. Pick a Subject image (image 1) — the person used across every scene. Pick or upload it from the dropdown.
  3. Optionally pick a Product image (image 2) — a branded product. It auto-binds to the scenes whose prompt names it. Leave empty if there's no product.
  4. Set aspect ratio, resolution, model, and variants per scene (see Settings & Models). An optional Prompt prefix is prepended to every scene — it defaults to quality rules.
  5. Click ✨ Create & Generate in the modal. The scenes are created and queued. (The top-bar 📋 Import only opens this modal.)

Reference chains

A scene can be built on top of an earlier scene by adding a reference_image: image_K field (or none). That links them parent → child. (Scene N still works for legacy 1:1 tables.)

Two ways to set a reference without hand-writing the field:

  • + Add Parent — a visual parent-slot picker inside the Import modal. Point a scene at the subject, the product, or an earlier scene.
  • 📤 Upload Reference Image — a left-panel button that adds your own image as a standalone reference node, outside scene-table import.
ℹ️
How scheduling works: scenes with no reference start generating right away. A scene that references an earlier one stays in draft until you pick a chosen variant of its parent — only then does it queue. So each scene in a chain is built from an approved predecessor.
💡
It's the same ### Scene N + prompt format used across the platform. Build the frames here, approve them, then promote to a video.

17 Image Worker (BYO)

Image generation runs through your own browser worker — the same pattern as the video Flow worker, pointed at Nano Banana. Find it in the left ⚙ Worker panel.

The panel shows Your image worker: ● Online or ● Offline plus the last heartbeat. When it's offline, the setup controls appear:

  1. Pick your OS: Windows or Mac / Linux.
  2. Choose Parallel slots — concurrent generations. 1 is sequential, 4 is the recommended max, 5–6 push it.
  3. Optional: Wipe Chrome session before start to force a fresh login.
  4. Optional Google login email (BYO): if your computer's Chrome is already signed into that Google account, the worker reuses that login — no verification code. Leave blank to log in manually.
  5. Click ⬇ Download Installer (.bat on Windows, .command on Mac).
  6. Run it: double-click the .bat (Windows), or unzip and double-click the .command (Mac — right-click → Open if macOS blocks it). Log into Google Flow once if prompted.
⚠️
Keep the worker running while images generate — keep the terminal window open. It picks up queued scenes automatically and stops when you close it.
ℹ️
If the status reads online but nothing generates, click Worker not responding? Set up / relaunch to re-open the installer and start a fresh worker.

Promote to Video

Once every scene is ready and has a chosen variant, the 🎬 Promote to video button appears. It stages the whole batch in the Videos tab for a last visual review before generating the talking-head video. 20 Local Folder

Connect a folder on your computer so finished videos get picked up automatically. Open the Local folder chip on the Jobs board.

  1. Click 📁 Pick local folder and choose a folder.
  2. While the tab is open, the app scans it every ~30 seconds for new .mp4 / .mov / .webm files.
  3. New files upload and auto-match to jobs in Awaiting Finishing, moving them to Published.
  4. 🔄 Force rescan runs a scan right now; Stop watching disconnects the folder.
ℹ️
Install KavenoBuilder as an app (Chrome address bar → install icon) so the folder permission sticks across sessions. Otherwise re-pick the folder once each session.
⚠️
Folder watching works in Chrome, Edge, and Opera only — Firefox and Safari can't watch local folders.

21 Instagram Tracking

Connect an Instagram account to track when your reels go live. Open the Instagram chip on the Jobs board.

  1. Click + Add account.
  2. Enter the @handle and your HikerAPI key, then Save.
  3. Sync pulls the account's recent posts.
  4. When a published job's reel is detected live, its 📷 Instagram link fills in on the job card automatically.
💡
A 📷 badge on a job card links straight to the posted reel.

22 Tips & Best Practices

📸 Use the right image

Well-lit, front-facing photos with clean backgrounds. Avoid heavy filters or unusual angles.

⏱️ Always use 8s + interpolation

The single biggest quality improvement. 8s gives the AI room for natural speech motion. 4s clips often look mechanical.

🖥️ Run 2+ Chrome Windows

Each window is a parallel worker account. Two or more process clips simultaneously — roughly the time of one for several clips.

✍️ Write like you talk

Contractions, natural sentence lengths, numbers as words ("twenty five"). The AI needs speech patterns, not written prose.

✅ Review as you go

Approve good clips immediately and Redo bad ones right away — redos process in parallel with remaining clips.

✂️ Keep Smart Trim on

Preserves natural openings for the first clip and scene cuts while trimming static frames at clip joins.

🔇 Audio enhancement

Turn on Remove Laughter and DeepFilterNet for cleaner audio — they're off by default, and your choice is remembered. Only enable Voice Filter if the audio specifically needs heavy compression.
