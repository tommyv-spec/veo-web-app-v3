Short Lines ?
📝 Short Dialogue Mode

How to handle dialogue lines under 13 words:

  • Timed: 2s speech + silent action for remaining time (default)
  • Fill: Pad with filler words to 25 words for continuous speech

Fill mode avoids awkward silence but may sound unnatural at the end.
Prefix Short ?
🔤 Prefix Short Lines

Prepend a leading word (e.g. "only") to dialogue lines shorter than the threshold.

Useful when Veo clips the first consonant of a clip's first word at scene boundaries — the prefix gives Veo a throwaway onset to "warm up" on, so the real first word survives the cut.

  • Stacks with Timed or Fill — independent setting
  • Threshold counts only the original line (default: under 15 words)
  • Prefix word and threshold are both customizable

Applied to BOTH the Veo prompt AND the WhisperVAD script — so silence-removal won't cut the prefix back out.

Gesture Enrichment ?
🤌 Gesture Enrichment

AI generates content-specific gestures matching what's being said.

  • Mentions head: points at head
  • Mentions mixing: stirring motion
  • Lists 3 things: holds up 3 fingers

Uses 1 extra OpenAI call per clip (~$0.001/clip).
Generation Mode ?
⚡ Generation Mode

Choose how clips are processed:

  • 🔀 Smart (Staggered): Odd clips (1,3,5) first in parallel, then even clips (2,4) use confirmed frames.
  • ⚡ Fast (Parallel): All clips at once. Fastest, but may have gaps.
  • 🔗 Safe (Sequential): One at a time. Slowest but most reliable.

📋 Import scene table

Paste a markdown scene table. Each ### Scene N block becomes a generated node. The reference_image field creates parent→child links between scenes. The subject you pick below is attached to every scene as the "image 1" subject.
How scheduling works: Scenes without a reference_image start queuing immediately. Scenes that reference an earlier scene stay in draft until you pick a chosen variant of their parent — only then do they auto-queue. This ensures each scene in a chain is built from an approved predecessor.
